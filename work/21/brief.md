## Acceptance criteria

- Worker tests, with a lease duration of a few seconds or a controlled clock: an `ok` result stores the transcript in R2 with its metadata and makes the job `done`; the same result sent again gives the same 200 and writes nothing; `no_transcript` is terminal; `unavailable`, `blocked` and `error` requeue the job for another node, and the third failure gives `failed_video`; an expired lease requeues the job, its late result gets 409 `stale_lease` and writes nothing; a lease that expires exactly at `lease_expires_at` is expired; each validation rule of 8.4 gives 422 and counts as a failure; a release undoes the claim and is idempotent; a revoked node gets 401 and its job is reassigned.
