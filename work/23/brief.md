## Acceptance criteria

- Tests against a local fake Worker (no real network): `--once` sends a heartbeat, a claim and one result per job with the right `outcome` and `transcript`; a pipeline exception sends `error`; a 503 on `/node/result` is retried and then succeeds; a 409 is not retried; a failed claim exits 1; a missing `NODE_TOKEN` exits 2; SIGTERM releases the claimed jobs not started and exits 0; standard error holds only JSON log lines, and no log line holds the token.
