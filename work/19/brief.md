## Context

Sections 7 (nodes and jobs), 8.1, 8.2, 8.3 and 8.9 of `spec/lot-2.md`: nodes are enrolled with a token shown once, jobs are queued by the operator, and nodes claim them under a lease, atomically.

## Decided spec

- `POST /ops/nodes`, `POST /ops/jobs`, `GET /ops/jobs/<video_id>` (section 7); the node token format and its SHA-256 storage (6.3).
- The node authentication of 8.1, `POST /node/heartbeat` (8.2) and `POST /node/claim` (8.3): eligibility, order, lease fields, `attempts` and `tried_nodes`, a `leases` row per claim, `LEASE_SECONDS`.
- Claims are decided by single conditional SQL statements or D1 batches (8.9).
- The log events `node.enrolled`, `node.heartbeat`, `job.enqueued`, `job.claimed` and `auth.bearer.rejected` (section 12).

## Acceptance criteria

- Worker tests: 401 without a bearer, with an unknown token and with the operator token on `/node/claim`; a heartbeat updates the node and answers `healthy`, `run`, 60; an empty queue gives `{"jobs": []}`; a claim without `max_jobs` takes 3; priority order then creation order; a claimed job shows `leased`, `attempts` 1 and the node in `tried_nodes`; a node already in `tried_nodes` is not given the job; enqueuing an existing job keeps it and raises its priority only upwards.
- A test runs concurrent claims of several nodes on the real local D1 and shows that no job is handed out twice and that each claim still gets `min(max_jobs, eligible jobs)` (8.3).
- An e2e test (`api` project): 2 nodes claim 6 jobs concurrently and each job is handed out once.
- `./scripts/check.sh all` passes.
